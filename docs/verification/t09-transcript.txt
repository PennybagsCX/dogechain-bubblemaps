  The explorer's 'Transaction fee' row equals the measured balance delta on all 9 txs, so the
  indexer knows the real fee while eth_getTransactionReceipt does not. Two lessons that cost time:
   - a deploy's price cannot be derived from gas: the node's pre-flight check (gasLimit * ~4.5e11
     wei) is the only pre-broadcast predictor, and it was within 3% of the real debit (0.1879 vs
     0.1935 DOGE) while the receipt was off by 494x.
   - when measuring a NATIVE transfer, subtract tx.value from the balance delta (the first pass of
     this transcript did not, and the explorer's fee row caught the error).
